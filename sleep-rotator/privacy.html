Sleep rotator: privacy policy

What it uses. With its owner's permission, Sleep rotator uses YouTube API Services to read and edit the owner's own YouTube playlists. It does not read anyone else's data.

What it keeps. Only the IDs of videos it has already placed, stored on the owner's own server, so a story is never repeated. It collects no names, email addresses or other personal data.

Sharing. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Stopping it. The owner can remove its access at any time in the Google Account, under Security, Third-party apps and services: myaccount.google.com/connections.

YouTube and Google. Sleep rotator uses YouTube API Services. Its use follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
